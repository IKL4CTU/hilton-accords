Department of Justice

Dedicated to the impartial administration of justice, the preservation of law and order, and the investigation of matters determined worthy of investigation by the Director.

Office of the Director

Director: Hefty

Director Hefty serves as the principal legal and investigative authority of the Hilton Accords, entrusted with safeguarding the integrity of government institutions.

In addition to his responsibilities within this Department, Director Hefty simultaneously oversees the Department of Armed Forces.

Questions concerning the independence of these offices have been reviewed by the Department of Justice.

"The Director has investigated the Director and determined that the Director has conducted himself appropriately."

Our Commitment to Justice

The Department maintains that justice must be administered with integrity, fairness, restraint, and respect for the principles of the Hilton Accords.

All persons appearing before the Department shall be afforded the opportunity to present their position, provided their position does not substantially inconvenience the Director.

Decisions shall be based upon available evidence, established procedures, and the Director's interpretation of both.

"Equal justice under law. Final interpretation reserved to the Office of the Director."

Official Case Registry

The Department maintains records of selected investigations and administrative proceedings.

Office of Public Complaints

The Department accepts fictional complaints concerning government operations, conduct, and matters of administrative concern.

Filing a complaint does not guarantee investigation, acknowledgment of wrongdoing, or satisfaction with the outcome.

Office of Judicial Appeals

Individuals dissatisfied with a final departmental determination may request reconsideration.

Such requests shall be independently reviewed by the appellate authority, Director Hefty.

A Message from Director Hefty

The Department of Justice stands committed to the highest standards of legal integrity and institutional responsibility.

As Director, I recognize the importance of accountability, due process, and independent oversight.

It is therefore my pleasure to announce that, following an exhaustive review of this administration, I have found no evidence of misconduct requiring further action.

I extend my congratulations to the Department, its Director, and the distinguished investigative authority responsible for these findings.

"Justice has been served. I have personally confirmed it."

Director Hefty
