Strategic Defense Doctrine

The Department of Armed Forces maintains that military preparedness exists to preserve peace, not to provoke conflict.

Operational discipline, restraint, and readiness shall guide all actions undertaken on behalf of the Hilton Accords.

Defense readiness shall remain a continuing responsibility regardless of prevailing threat conditions.

"We do not seek conflict. We prepare so that peace may endure."
